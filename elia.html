Elia

At ELIA, I worked on GreenTracking, a strategic software initiative designed to track and manage green certificates within one of Belgium’s leading energy companies.

In this role I:

  • Collaborated closely with a program manager to successfully launch a new software solution from concept to production.
  • Designed and implemented a new user interface, with a strong emphasis on ergonomics, clarity, and usability for business and operational users.
  • Contributed to the overall system architecture, ensuring scalability, reliability, and maintainability in a production-critical environment.
  • Integrated asynchronous processing and messaging to support background jobs and data workflows.

The solution was developed using C# (.NET 5 / .NET 6), Blazor, Entity Framework, SQL, NSwagger, Highcharts, Hangfire, Task Scheduler, and RabbitMQ, leveraging modern .NET practices to deliver a robust and user-friendly energy-sector application.
