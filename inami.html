RIZIV - INAMI

At INAMI, I contributed to the development of Practice Register, a large-scale platform designed to manage the careers, accreditation, and professional data of healthcare workers at a national level.

In this role I:

  • Contributed to the creation of a high-volume, mission-critical platform used by healthcare professionals and public institutions.
  • Designed and optimized the database architecture, improving database team efficiency by 30% through clearer data models and performance improvements.
  • Worked closely with UX/UI designers, streamlining collaboration workflows and reducing design-related effort by 50%, while ensuring a coherent and user-friendly interface.
  • Actively contributed to code quality and reliability through automated testing and modern development practices.

The platform was built using C# (.NET 6), Blazor WebAssembly, Entity Framework, SQL, Keycloak for authentication, xUnit for testing, and Telerik UI components, within the constraints and quality standards of the healthcare and public sector.
